Maximum two shouts per match. Never stack shouts. Note: this two-shouts-per-match rule is our internal operating discipline, NOT a confirmed AFC competition rule. If the current portal confirms a different restriction, we follow the portal and record what it says.2. Observation before intervention. Shouts are written in the same action vocabulary as V2 (Pass, Shoot, Press_Ball, Mark, Intercept, Clear) so they read cleanly into the agents' decision context.---## The eight shouts### S1 - RESTORE SHAPE (restore compact shape)- Trigger: Opponent repeatedly cutting through central areas - more than one central breach in a short spell, or defenders drawn out of the block.- Exact shout (91 chars): `Hold shape. Stay compact. Only the nearest player presses. Everyone else recovers position.`- Intended effect: Raises the press threshold back to V2's "nearest appropriate player presses"; collapses everyone back into shape.- Cancel / replace evidence: Two consecutive clean defensive possessions with no central breach → cancel. If we fall behind chasing the game → replace with S3.- Main risk: Concedes territory and tempo while the shape resets.- Telemetry: Central breaches per 10 minutes vs the pre-shout rate; command mix shifting from Press_Ball toward Mark / Intercept.### S2 - PROTECT THE LEAD (protect a lead)- Trigger: We lead by one goal and the opponent's attacking tempo is rising (shots or final-third entries increasing).- Exact shout (91 chars): `Protect the lead. Stay compact. Look forward only when clearly safe. No risks in our third.`- Intended effect: Suppresses risky build-up and forward gambles while keeping V2's compact base. Note the scoring logic: wins carry the bonuses, so a held lead is the highest-value action on the pitch.- Cancel / replace evidence: If we concede → replace with S3. If the cushion reaches two goals and pressure fades → cancel.- Main risk: Invites a sustained siege; hurried clearances hand the ball straight back.- Telemetry: Shots conceded per 10 minutes; defensive-third turnovers; share of time spent defending in our third.### S3 - RAISE THE PRESS (increase pressure when chasing) - CORRECTED- Trigger: One goal down with meaningful time remaining.- Exact shout (113 chars): `One down. Raise the block. Nearest player presses high; others cover. Look forward fast. Shoot when a lane opens.`- Intended effect: Converts V2's selective press into a coordinated high press while preserving the nearest-player pressing principle - only the nearest player presses high; the others hold their cover rather than independently chasing the ball. Also accelerates progression.- Cancel / replace evidence: After scoring → S2. Defensive-third turnovers spiking → cancel and revert to V2 pressing rules.- Main risk: Space behind the press - counterattack exposure.- Telemetry: Final-third recoveries; progressive-pass attempts; turnovers by zone; shots; Press_Ball share of commands (checks the nearest-player principle is holding - if multiple agents press at once, the shout is working against us).### S4 - PLAY THROUGH THE PRESS (exploit space against a high press)- Trigger: Opponent pressing high repeatedly; clear space behind their midfield; our agents repeatedly passing backward or clearing under pressure.- Exact shout (92 chars): `Their press is high. Space is behind them. Play early forward passes. Run into the channels.`- Intended effect: Shifts the distribution decision forward and triggers channel runs into the vacated space.- Cancel / replace evidence: Two clean progressive possessions out of our third → cancel. If interceptions against us spike → cancel.- Main risk: Ambitious passes under pressure become turnovers that lead to shots against us.- Telemetry: Progressive-pass success rate; interceptions in our half; attacks started from our defensive third.### S5 - BREAK THE BLOCK (break down a deep opponent) - CORRECTED- Trigger: Opponent camped in its own half; our shot count low; possession sterile around the box.- Exact shout (115 chars): `They sit deep. Use wide lanes. Pass into the box or cut back when open. Shoot from the edge when the lane is clear.`- Intended effect: Attacks a compact block through the wide lanes - using passes into the box, cut-backs and edge shots that are all directly expressible through the available action set (no dedicated cross command exists). Encourages earlier shots from the edge rather than forcing through the middle.- Cancel / replace evidence: Shot volume rises but conversion stays poor across the match → cancel. If we take the lead → S2.- Main risk: Wasteful possession; intercepted passes into the box launch their counters.- Telemetry: Shot volume and locations; goals per attacking move; shots conceded immediately after our attacking possession breaks down.### S6 - STOP THE CHASE (stop excessive pressing / chasing)- Trigger: Multiple agents pressing at once - central space exposed, opponent playing through us. The mirror image of V2's pressing rule being broken.- Exact shout (90 chars): `Stop chasing. Hold positions. Only the nearest player presses. Everyone else covers space.`- Intended effect: Re-anchors the V2 pressing rule and ends collective chasing.- Cancel / replace evidence: Command mix returns to normal and central breaches stop → cancel.- Main risk: Passive defending lets the opponent dictate tempo unopposed.- Telemetry: Press_Ball share of commands; central breaches; opponent sustained-attack length.### S7 - HOLD THE OUTLET (preserve a counterattacking outlet)- Trigger: Opponent committing numbers forward; risk of our forwards collapsing onto the ball and killing our counter outlet.- Exact shout (95 chars): `Stay wide, stay high. Hold the outlet. Win it back, then pass forward fast. Do not over-commit.`- Intended effect: Keeps Forward 2 (and Forward 1) wide and high so turnovers turn into counters - consistent with V2's wide-outlet role for Forward 2.- Cancel / replace evidence: After scoring → S2. When the opponent retreats and we dominate possession → cancel.- Main risk: Forwards isolated - if midfield cannot win the ball, the outlet becomes spectators.- Telemetry: Counter-attacking shots within seconds of recoveries; forward average position and spacing.### S8 - ALL-OUT FINAL PUSH (emergency final attacking phase) - CORRECTED- Trigger: Behind and running out of time in the final phase - or level in the dying moments deliberately hunting a winner.- Exact shout (139 chars): `Final push. Outfield players take more attacking positions. Nearest player presses high. Shoot early from clear lanes. Keeper stays secure.`- Intended effect: The one shout that deliberately overrides V2's pressing rule and compactness - outfield players push forward while the nearest player presses high, and the goalkeeper explicitly stays secure because the shout reaches every agent on the pitch, keeper included. Accepted in exchange for maximum goals-per-minute.- Cancel / replace evidence: After scoring → immediately S2 (do not celebrate into a loss). Otherwise the match ends.- Main risk: Reduced defensive coverage as outfield players push up. Deliberately accepted when behind; only taken when level if the boss calls it.- Telemetry: Shots in the final phase; final-third recoveries; goalkeeper safety (no keeper-induced concession in the dying minutes); how many agents actually pushed forward - that is the direct measure of shout influence.---## Observation vs interventionShouting is for the moments the agents cannot see: score state, clock, opponent pattern shifts. The agents see the game state every ~2 seconds - they do not need us to tell them what they can see. If the scorecard shows Shadow Wings playing correctly at 0-0, the correct action is to record and watch, not shout.
