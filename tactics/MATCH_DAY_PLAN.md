# Shadow Wings Match-Day Plan

Built on Prompt Set V2 (**FROZEN**) and Shout Playbook V1.1 (**FROZEN**).

## Scoring caution

Transferred KB Rev 21 material recorded a +10 clean-sheet bonus, but the current official AFC leaderboard page says **Clean match = +5 points**. The value is disputed.

The tactical principle below does not depend on choosing one value: protecting a lead is valuable; do not expose the team merely to chase an uncertain bonus.

## State 1 — Normal 0-0

- Let V2 operate.
- No shout by default.
- Watch turnovers in our defensive third, goalkeeper distribution and F1/F2 spacing.

## State 2 — Taking the lead

- No automatic tactical change.
- Record how the goal was created.
- Observe opponent response before intervening.

## State 3 — Protecting a narrow lead

- Protect the central route.
- If the opponent's pressure is clearly rising and shape is being lost, use **S1 RESTORE SHAPE** or **S2 PROTECT THE LEAD** according to the exact trigger in the frozen playbook.
- Watch defender stepping, goalkeeper rushing and midfield central coverage.

## State 4 — One goal behind

- If the trigger is met, use **S3 RAISE THE PRESS** once.
- Preserve Forward 2's outlet lane.
- Do not convert the whole team into ball-chasers.

## State 5 — Chasing late

- If behind in the final phase, **S8 ALL-OUT FINAL PUSH** is the emergency state.
- The goalkeeper remains secure.
- Log exact time, score and effect.

## State 6 — Opponent high press

- V2 already prefers safe release under dangerous pressure.
- If repeated defensive-third turnovers show the problem is real, use **S4 PLAY THROUGH THE PRESS**.
- Repeated failure becomes V2.1 evidence, not repeated improvisation.

## State 7 — Opponent deep block

- If possession is sterile and shots are not being created, use **S5 BREAK THE BLOCK** when its trigger is met.
- Do not invent a CROSS action.

## State 8 — Opponent controls central space

- Use **S1 RESTORE SHAPE** only when repeated central breaches meet its trigger.
- Log each breach for post-match classification.

## State 9 — Defensive emergency

- Keeper and defender V2 rules already prioritize Clear when immediate danger is high.
- No invented emergency shout outside the frozen playbook.

## State 10 — Final phase

- Ahead: protect shape; S2 if its trigger is met.
- Behind: S8.
- Level: do not force an all-out push unless the coach explicitly accepts the defensive trade-off and the S8 trigger is met.

## Shout discipline

- Maximum two shouts per match is an internal Shadow Wings rule, not a confirmed AFC platform limit.
- Never stack shouts intentionally while persistence/stacking remains TBC.
- Every shout is logged as a match variable.
- Isolated mistakes do not move V2. Only repeatable evidence does.
