# Keep / Revert FrameworkKEEP the change only when:1. The intended success metric improved as hypothesised, across sufficient repeat evidence (minimum two matches, practice or league), AND2. No guard metric degraded across those matches (latency, defensive-third turnovers, central breaches, shot conversion).REVERT when:1. The target metric fails to improve, OR2. An important guard metric degrades, OR3. The improvement cannot reasonably be attributed to the change (another variable changed, opponent quality differed materially) - retest clean, OR4. A technical regression appears.After KEEP: the change is absorbed; the version becomes the new baseline (V2.1, then V2.2...) and the next change starts from it.After REVERT: return to the previous baseline verbatim; log the hypothesis in experiments/CHANGELOG.md.Do not call reverted tests failures. Store them as useful experimental evidence - a failed hypothesis narrows the search.Every change gets a follow-up entry after its test window: what changed, what the data showed, kept or reverted, and what it teaches about this squad.
